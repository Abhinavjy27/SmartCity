# Knowledge Base Pre-Change Mismatches Log (kb_mismatches.md)

Prior to integration, the Planning AI (`frontend/src/pages/Planning.jsx` and default supervisor fallback) had no connection to the pollution domain telemetry or models. Every question received a hardcoded traffic signal optimization response regardless of the query.

### Question 10: "Why is PM2.5 or PM10 required to compute AQI?"
- **Path Before Change**: Returned traffic corridor efficiency advice.
- **Verified Ground Truth**: CPCB NAQI standard mandates at least one particulate matter pollutant (PM2.5 or PM10) because particulate matter is the primary public health risk in Indian urban centers. Without it, AQI is undefined.
